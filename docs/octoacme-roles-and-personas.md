# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers research user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences through interaction and visual design.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or contribute to user research and synthesize findings
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs through usability feedback and iterate on findings
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations for delivery

### Goals
- Make product experiences useful, understandable, and accessible
- Ground design decisions in user needs and evidence
- Give delivery teams clear, implementable design guidance

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- Prototypes, design specifications, and usability feedback
- Collaboration during discovery, implementation, and acceptance

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and priorities.
- Work with Developers and Engineering Leads to assess feasibility and clarify design handoffs.
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to include usability and accessibility in validation.
- Coordinate research, reviews, and dependencies with Project Managers; involve Stakeholders for relevant domain input and feedback.

---

## Engineering Leads / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical design and implementation coordination so the team can deliver reliable solutions aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate technical design and document important implementation decisions
- Identify engineering dependencies, estimates, and technical risks
- Support consistent engineering practices, code quality, and technical readiness
- Help coordinate implementation across Developers or teams

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable solutions that meet functional and quality needs
- Surface technical constraints and risks early
- Enable effective engineering collaboration and predictable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs, decision records, and code reviews
- Dependency, estimate, and risk updates
- Implementation and release-readiness discussions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate sequencing, dependencies, and technical risks with Project Managers.
- Work with Product Managers to explain technical trade-offs and clarify scope options.
- Support Developers through design decisions and implementation reviews.
- Partner with QA/Testing on testability and technical readiness, and communicate relevant constraints to Stakeholders.

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on protecting systems and data and on meeting applicable security and privacy expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy requirements and review relevant risks
- Advise on threat assessment, safeguards, and data handling
- Coordinate or recommend security and privacy validation
- Raise material risks and advise on mitigation or escalation

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks early in delivery
- Help teams apply appropriate safeguards and verification
- Make risk ownership and release concerns visible

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy reviews and risk-register updates
- Recommendations for controls and validation
- Escalations and release-readiness input

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advise Developers and Engineering Leads on requirements, design risks, and mitigations.
- Work with QA/Testing to verify security- and privacy-related acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate review timing and risk escalation with Project Managers, and discuss product trade-offs with Product Managers.
- Inform Stakeholders of material risks and decisions through the agreed communication path.

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help teams define and measure outcomes through reliable instrumentation, data definitions, and analysis.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success measures into clear metric definitions and data needs
- Plan instrumentation and coordinate event or reporting requirements
- Check data quality and document assumptions or limitations
- Prepare analysis that supports evaluation and informed decisions

### Goals
- Make project outcomes measurable and interpretable
- Provide trustworthy data for evaluating impact
- Identify measurement gaps early enough to address them

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and instrumentation plans
- Data-quality findings and dashboards or reports
- Outcome reviews with product and project teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align measures and interpretation with Product Managers and the Project One-pager.
- Coordinate instrumentation with Developers and Engineering Leads, and work with QA/Testing to verify tracked events and reporting.
- Share progress and outcome signals with Project Managers for status and planning.
- Explain findings and limitations to Stakeholders; coordinate with Security and Privacy Leads where measurement involves sensitive data.

---

## Customer Support / Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Representatives bring customer-facing insight into delivery and help prepare teams to support changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, feedback, and support trends
- Identify customer-impact considerations for proposed changes
- Prepare or update support guidance and communicate relevant release impacts
- Route customer-reported issues through agreed triage and escalation paths

### Goals
- Improve product decisions with customer and operational context
- Help customers and support teams adopt changes successfully
- Ensure customer-impacting issues are visible and routed promptly

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and support trend summaries
- Release notes, FAQs, and enablement materials
- Issue reports and post-release feedback

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Share customer needs and feedback with Product Managers to inform discovery and prioritization.
- Coordinate support readiness and release communications with Project Managers.
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify reported issues, customer impact, and reproduction or validation needs.
- Keep Stakeholders informed of relevant customer impact and readiness concerns.

---

## Assigning Personas

- These personas describe responsibilities, not required team structure; one person may cover multiple roles, and a role may be shared across a team.
- Agree on ownership and handoffs for each project based on its scope, risks, and team capabilities.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
