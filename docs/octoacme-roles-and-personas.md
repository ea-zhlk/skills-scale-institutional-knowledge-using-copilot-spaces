# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Engineers own quality assurance across the delivery lifecycle. They define test strategies, execute manual and automated tests, validate acceptance criteria, and drive defect resolution to ensure releases meet quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans, test cases, and acceptance test suites
- Execute manual and automated tests (functional, regression, integration, exploratory)
- Identify, document, and track defects to resolution
- Partner with Developers to ensure testability of features
- Gate releases by signing off on acceptance criteria before deployment
- Participate in sprint planning to assess testability and flag QA risks early

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Increase confidence in each release through thorough test coverage
- Reduce time spent on post-release bug fixes

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Developers daily on defect triage and test coverage
- Coordinate with PdM to clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases
- Report test status and release readiness to PM during weekly delivery syncs
- Contribute to the Release Checklist and Definition of Done

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure that features meet usability standards and serve user needs. They collaborate on requirements, produce wireframes and prototypes, and validate designs with users and stakeholders before development begins.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and translate findings into design requirements
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Review and refine acceptance criteria with PdM to capture usability requirements
- Partner with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Run usability tests and incorporate feedback into iterative designs
- Maintain a shared design system and component library

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and visually consistent user experiences
- Reduce rework by validating designs before development starts
- Ensure usability requirements are reflected in acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Weekly design reviews with PdM and engineering lead
- Share prototypes and research findings with the full team in sprint reviews
- Collaborate directly with Developers during implementation via design handoff tools
- Provide design sign-off before features are released

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers own user-facing and internal technical documentation. They ensure that documentation is accurate, timely, and aligned with released features, and they coordinate doc reviews across roles.

### Responsibilities
- Author and maintain user guides, API documentation, release notes, and internal runbooks
- Coordinate doc reviews with Developers, PdM, and QA Engineer before each release
- Track documentation gaps and open tickets for missing or outdated content
- Ensure release notes accurately reflect what shipped and are published on schedule
- Establish and maintain a doc review checklist used by the whole team

### Goals
- Ensure every release is accompanied by accurate, complete documentation
- Reduce support tickets caused by missing or unclear documentation
- Make it easy for new team members to onboard through clear internal docs

### Typical Communication
- Participate in sprint planning to identify documentation work for each feature
- Review PRs for documentation updates alongside code changes
- Coordinate with Support Engineer to identify and close documentation gaps surfaced by customers
- Sync with PdM on release content and messaging before each release

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business requirements and technical solutions. They validate that implemented work meets business needs and facilitate communication between stakeholders and the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Translate business requirements into user stories and acceptance criteria
- Validate that delivered features satisfy business objectives
- Facilitate workshops and discovery sessions with stakeholders
- Identify process gaps and recommend improvements

### Goals
- Ensure the team builds the right thing by clearly articulating business needs
- Reduce requirement ambiguity and mid-sprint scope changes
- Bridge communication gaps between technical and non-technical stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate closely with PdM on roadmap and backlog refinement
- Facilitate requirement sessions with business stakeholders
- Provide clarification to Developers and QA Engineer during implementation
- Review demos and acceptance tests to confirm business requirements are met

---

## Support Engineer / Customer Success

### Role Summary
Support Engineers and Customer Success Managers are the voice of the customer inside the team. They surface user feedback, escalate critical pain points, and ensure the team is prepared to support customers through each release.

### Responsibilities
- Act as first-line responders for customer issues and escalate critical bugs to the engineering team
- Collect and synthesize user feedback and share it with PdM and the delivery team
- Participate in release readiness reviews to ensure support materials are ready
- Author and maintain internal knowledge base articles and FAQs
- Coordinate with Technical Writer on customer-facing release notes and help content
- Track recurring issues and advocate for systemic fixes on the roadmap

### Goals
- Minimize customer impact from bugs and unclear features
- Ensure the support team is prepared for every release
- Close the feedback loop between customers and the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Weekly sync with PdM to share customer feedback and escalate high-impact issues
- Participate in release readiness calls with PM, QA Engineer, and Technical Writer
- Coordinate with Technical Writer to ensure help documentation is accurate and published before release
- Report on support ticket trends and top customer pain points in retrospectives
